# How did the trial-to-subscription rate on TikTok change month over month?

## Reference answer

It held roughly steady: **40.1% in September and 38.9% in October so far.**

| Month | Trials | Converted | Trial → sub |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September (from Sep 3) | 10,512 | 4,215 | 40.1% |
| October (Oct 1–10) | 3,977 | 1,549 | 38.9% |

October only counts trials that started by Oct 10. Later trials haven't reached the end of the 3-day trial yet; including them would show October at 30.5%, which is a timing effect, not a real drop.
