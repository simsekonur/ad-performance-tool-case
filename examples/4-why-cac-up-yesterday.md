# Why did CAC go up yesterday?

## Reference answer

**It didn't really: yesterday's subscriptions haven't arrived yet.** CAC reads $200.25 yesterday against $27.62 for Oct 5–11. But trials last 3 days, so most of yesterday's subscriptions come in over the next few days.

Cost per trial is the fair comparison for a day this recent: $12.23 yesterday against $12.02 for Oct 5–11, essentially flat. Nothing needs action; check CAC again in 3–4 days.
