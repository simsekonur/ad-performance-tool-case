# Is TikTok cheaper than Meta for us right now?

## Reference answer

**No. TikTok only looks cheaper because most of its budget is in Brazil.** Over the last two complete weeks (Sep 28 – Oct 11), blended CAC is $20.28 on TikTok and $35.49 on Meta. But 70.2% of TikTok's spend is in Brazil, where every subscriber costs less. Country by country, Meta is cheaper everywhere, and it earns more back:

| Country | Meta CAC | TikTok CAC | Meta D7 ROAS | TikTok D7 ROAS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US | $46.73 | $52.48 | 86.9% | 71.2% |
| DE | $44.35 | $49.56 | 78.9% | 67.5% |
| BR | $14.27 | $16.14 | 80.9% | 64.8% |
| GB | $35.05 | — | 103.5% | — |

Overall D7 ROAS is 87.9% on Meta and 66.3% on TikTok.
