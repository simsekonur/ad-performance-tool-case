# Glossary

How the Livensa marketing team talks about its numbers.

## Account

- **Channels:** Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok.
- **Structure:** campaign → ad set → ad. TikTok calls ad sets "ad groups"; here they are all ad sets.
- **Countries:** each campaign targets one country: US, GB, DE or BR.
- **Today** is 2026-10-15. "Yesterday" is 2026-10-14, the last day in the warehouse.

## Spend

- **Spend:** what the ad network charges, in USD.

## Funnel

- **Install:** a first app open attributed to an ad.
- **Trial:** a user who starts the free trial.
- **Trial conversion:** a trial that becomes paid when the trial ends.
- **Subscription:** a new paying user, whether through a trial or directly.
- **D7 revenue:** revenue from a user in their first 7 days.

Trials, trial conversions, subscriptions and D7 revenue are counted on the install day of the user they came from. Recent days keep filling in for about a week as trials end and renewals come in.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| CPI | Spend ÷ installs |
| CAC | Spend ÷ subscriptions |
| Cost per trial | Spend ÷ trials |
| Trial → sub | Trial conversions ÷ trials |
| D7 ROAS | D7 revenue ÷ spend |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ impressions |
