# Team notes

How the Livensa marketing team likes its questions answered. The agent should follow these; the team adds new ones over time.

1. **"Last week"** means the previous Monday to Sunday. **"Last N days"** means the N full days ending yesterday.
2. **Money** is in USD with two decimals. **Percentages** have one decimal.
3. **Lead with the answer** in one sentence, then the numbers behind it.
4. **Name things the way the team does:** ad sets and ads by their names, never by their ids.
5. **CAC for days less than 4 days old isn't final**, because trials are still converting. Say so, and show cost per trial for those days instead.
6. **Don't call an ad or ad set the best or worst** until it has at least 20 subscriptions in the period. Below that, say the sample is too small.
7. **Compare channels within each country first**, then give the blended number. Most of TikTok's spend is in Brazil, so blended numbers alone mislead.
8. **Paused ads** count in spend history, but leave them out of recommendations about what to scale or cut.
